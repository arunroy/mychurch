Using the app

You must be at least 13 years old. Use your real name so your church family knows who you are, keep your sign-in email secure, and do not share your account.

Each church sets its own community. A church's leaders decide who is approved to join, and they can remove a member.

What you post

You are responsible for what you write and share. You keep ownership of it, and you allow the app to show it to the people you share it with in your church.

Do not post anything that is hateful, harassing, threatening, sexually explicit, or illegal, that bullies or targets a person, or that shares private information about someone without their permission.

Only share articles and sermons you wrote yourself or have permission to share. For sermons by other people, share a link to the original rather than copying their text.

Moderation

Church leaders can remove content and members in their church. The Pastor reviews articles and outside sermon links before other members can see them, and can decline them.

You can report content you think breaks these terms. We may remove content, or suspend or remove accounts, that do.

Anonymous features

Anonymous messages and questions are for honest questions, concerns and care. They are limited each day, and they must still follow these terms. Leaders can remove an anonymous question, but because nothing says who wrote it, they cannot tell who it was.

Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time from More, then Delete my account. We or your church's leaders may also end an account that breaks these terms.
